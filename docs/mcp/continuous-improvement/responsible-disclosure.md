# Responsible Disclosure

Security reports should be sent to `security@flownode.com`.

## What To Include

- A short description of the issue.
- A safe reproduction path.
- The affected endpoint, MCP tool, OAuth flow, widget, or dashboard surface.
- Impact assessment using the severity matrix.
- Screenshots or sanitized logs when useful.

Do not include live credentials, raw API keys, OAuth tokens, private workflow payloads, or customer data in the first report.

## Response Targets

| Severity | Triage Target | Fix Target |
|---|---:|---:|
| Critical | 1 business day | 7 days |
| High | 2 business days | 14 days |
| Medium | 5 business days | 30 days |
| Low | 10 business days | Next planned cycle |

## Safe Harbor

Good-faith testing is welcome when it avoids privacy harm, service disruption, data destruction, spam, social engineering, and access to accounts or data that are not yours.
